User guide

How to use this app

An unofficial study aid for Duff's Elementary New Testament Greek. Flip cards, mark how confident you felt, and the app schedules what comes back next.

Advanced review settings

Tucked under Advanced review settings on the home screen. All toggles persist across sessions.

  • Shuffle — randomize card order in the current deck.
  • Required only — hide optional / supplemental words and study only course‑required vocabulary. On by default.
  • Eng → Gk — flip the prompt direction (English shown first, you recall the Greek). Works in both vocab and grammar modes.
  • Spaced review — turn spaced repetition on or off. On by default.
  • Self‑check (grammar mode only) — hide the answer until you tap to reveal, then rate yourself.
  • Reshuffle — re‑order whatever cards are currently due/eligible without changing your progress.
  • Reset deck — clear marks and progress for the currently selected sets only.
  • Reset stats — wipe all study statistics globally. There is no undo.

How spaced repetition works

Each card has its own schedule. When you mark a card the app adjusts both when it returns and an ease factor that scales future intervals:

  • ✗ Hard — comes back within minutes, drops ease and steps the card back one stage.
  • ~ Uncertain — comes back at roughly half its previous interval (capped between 1 hour and 1 week), small ease decrease.
  • ✓ Easy — advances to the next ramp step (~1 → 3 → 7 → 14 days, then growing), increases ease, and recent accuracy further multiplies the gap.

With Spaced review off, the deck behaves as a simple flip deck: Hard and Uncertain shuffle the card to the back of the active pile (it'll reappear later in the same pass), and Easy archives the card. When every card in the current pass has been marked, the still-active cards reshuffle into a fresh pass. Archived cards stay archived until you press ↻ Reset (mid-deck the button asks for confirmation; at the end of a deck the Next arrow itself becomes a one-tap Reset) or pick a new session. All three marks still contribute to your confidence/analytics. Use the ↶ Undo button under the card to reverse your last mark in spaced mode.

Tips

  • Be honest with Hard / Uncertain / Easy — the schedule only helps if it reflects what you actually knew.
  • Switch to the Memorization page when you want to study paradigm tables side‑by‑side rather than one card at a time.
  • The Reader's translation drills are gentler than raw verses — work through the drills for a chapter before tackling its TR verses.
  • Theme (System / Dark / Light) is in the top‑right of the header.
Before you begin

Please read before using this app

This study tool was made with AI by a student. It is unofficial and may contain mistakes, omissions, or misleading answers.

Always check vocabulary, parsing, grammar explanations, and quiz content against your course content, instructor guidance, and official materials.

This app is meant to help with review and memorization, not to replace assigned coursework or authoritative sources.

Quick start: flip a card, then mark Hard, Uncertain, or Easy.

Reset spaced review

What would you like to reset?

Set all to now keeps your SRS progress (streaks, intervals, ease) but makes every card in this deck due immediately, so you can review them all right away.

Reset progress clears all SRS scheduling and progress for this deck. Pass history and unspaced marks are kept.

Reset stats

What would you like to reset?

Reset stats clears all saved study stats, marks, spaced-review scheduling, achievements, and study-time history. Your settings (theme, fonts, profile, disclaimer) are kept.

Reset to start wipes everything and returns the app to its initial launch state — stats, settings, theme, fonts, profile, and the study-aid disclaimer are all cleared, and the page will reload.

Reset deck

Reset this flip deck?

Brings every archived card back into rotation and reshuffles the deck. Your spaced-review scheduling, intervals, confidence history, and pass history are kept.
